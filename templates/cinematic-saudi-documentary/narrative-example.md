# narrative-example.md — worked example narrative

Example only — a placeholder story shape for a real project's checkpoint
assets, not a claim about any specific place, person, or statistic. A real
project rewrites every line against its own verified checkpoint
(`asset-checkpoint-template.md`) and sources.

12 sentences (within the 10–15 range), simple Arabic, no tashkeel, one
visual beat per sentence, flowing past → identity → people → transformation
→ present → future → closing, per `TEMPLATE_RULES.md` §2.

## narrative.md (example)

من قلب الجزيرة العربية، بدأت حكاية أرض قديمة.
الرمال حفظت أثر قوافل مرت من هنا منذ قرون.
هنا تشكلت هوية لم تتغير رغم مرور الزمن.
اللغة والعادات توارثتها الأجيال دون انقطاع.
في كل مدينة وقرية، يعيش ناس يحملون هذا الإرث.
أيدي الحرفيين ما زالت تصنع ما تعلمته من الآباء.
ثم جاء وقت التغيير، بخطى واثقة لا متسرعة.
المدن ارتفعت، والطرق امتدت، والمشاريع تعددت.
اليوم تقف المملكة بين إرثها وطموحها.
الشباب يبني، والعالم يراقب هذا التحول عن قرب.
الغد يكتب الآن، بخطوات مبنية على أساس متين.
أرض واحدة، جذور راسخة، ومستقبل لم يكتمل بعد.

## Beat map

Sentence → shot / timing / visual purpose. `shot` names an asset-checkpoint
slot, not a real filename — a real project fills this from its own
checkpoint. `timing` assumes a 40s cut (`scene-map-example.md` uses these
exact numbers).

| # | Sentence (first words) | Flow stage | Shot (checkpoint slot) | Timing | Visual purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | من قلب الجزيرة... | past/roots | wide archival desert landscape, still | 0.0–3.0s | establish place, frame-0 hook |
| 2 | الرمال حفظت أثر... | past/roots | archival caravan/trade-route photo, still | 3.0–6.0s | ground the past claim in an image |
| 3 | هنا تشكلت هوية... | identity | historic architecture detail, still | 6.0–9.0s | identity through built form |
| 4 | اللغة والعادات... | identity | calligraphy or majlis scene, footage | 9.0–12.5s | identity through practice, not text |
| 5 | في كل مدينة وقرية... | people | wide of a town/village street, footage | 12.5–16.0s | shift scale from land to people |
| 6 | أيدي الحرفيين... | people | close-up craftsman hands, footage | 16.0–19.5s | humanize, close framing |
| 7 | ثم جاء وقت التغيير... | transformation (pivot) | dissolve from craft to construction, footage | 19.5–23.0s | pivot beat — short dissolve transition |
| 8 | المدن ارتفعت... | transformation | skyline/infrastructure, footage | 23.0–26.5s | scale of change, single shot only |
| 9 | اليوم تقف المملكة... | present | modern public space, footage | 26.5–30.0s | present-day anchor |
| 10 | الشباب يبني... | present/future | young professionals or builders, footage | 30.0–33.5s | people carry the transformation forward |
| 11 | الغد يكتب الآن... | future | forward-leaning wide shot (road, horizon, skyline under light), footage | 33.5–37.0s | future without literal futurism/CGI |
| 12 | أرض واحدة... | closing | return to a land/place still, still | 37.0–40.0s | closing statement, echoes shot 1 |

Notes:
- Shot 7 is the one narrative pivot — the only place `TEMPLATE_RULES.md` §5
  suggests a short dissolve; every other cut is a hard cut or sound-led.
- Shot 12 deliberately echoes shot 1 (land) to close the loop without
  restating any sentence.
- No sentence names a statistic, date, or superlative — this example stays
  inside `../../CONTENT_RULES.md` §Accuracy even as a placeholder.
